# Private Rooms

Private rooms allow you to play ranked games or a variety of other modes without your elo being at risk. There are a large number of customization options available.

# How Does it Work?

Under the "Start a Match" button, when you click "Create Room," you are put into a lobby, containing a list of players in your room, a settings page, a code you can give to your friends, and a "Start" button when you would like to start a game. To invite someone, simply copy the code at the top of the screen, and give it to your friend. They can then hit the "Join Room" button in the "Start Match" page, and paste the code in to join.

# In-Game Options

The room leader or co-leader may seed change without needing approval from any others. They may also force end a match after forfeiting themselves.
